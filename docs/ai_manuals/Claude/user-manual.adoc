== Introduction

=== What Is This Application?

The *Asset Booking Management* system is an internal web application developed for
Maurer Electronics employees. It provides a simple, unified interface for discovering,
reserving, and managing workplace assets — including laptops, parking spots, desks,
meeting rooms, books, and IT equipment.

The system ensures that no asset can be double-booked, gives employees real-time
visibility of asset availability, and allows managers to approve or reject booking
requests where required.

=== Who Is This Manual For?

This manual is intended for all Maurer Electronics employees who need to:

* Browse and search available workplace assets
* Make, view, or cancel reservations
* Manage their account profile

A separate section covers functionality available only to users with the *Admin* role
(user management and category management).

=== How to Access the Application

The application is accessible through any modern web browser. No installation is required.

. Open your web browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge recommended).
. Navigate to the internal application URL provided by your IT department.
. You will be presented with the login screen.

NOTE: The application is also available as a mobile app for booking assets via QR code.
Contact your IT department for the mobile app link.

== Getting Started

=== Logging In

image::LoginPage.png[Login screen — Prijava]

. On the login screen, enter your *Korisničko ime* (username) in the first field.
. Enter your *Lozinka* (password) in the second field.
. Click the *PRIJAVA* (Login) button.

NOTE: Your username and password are the same credentials used for your Maurer
corporate account. If you do not have login credentials, contact your manager or the IT department.

If you do not yet have an account, click the *"Nemate račun? Registrirajte se ovdje."*
link at the bottom of the login card to request registration.

The application supports both *light and dark mode*, as well as *language switching*
(Croatian/English), using the controls in the top-right corner of every page.

=== First-Time Login

No additional setup is required after your first login. Your account is pre-configured
by your system administrator with the appropriate role and department.

After logging in you will be taken directly to the main application.

=== Logging Out

To log out of the application:

. Locate your name at the bottom of the left-hand navigation sidebar.
. Click *Odjava* (Logout) below your name.

You will be returned to the login screen.

== Browsing and Searching Assets

=== The Reservations Page

image::Reservations.png[Reservations page — asset category tiles and asset list]

The *Rezervacije* (Reservations) page is the main starting point for finding and
booking an asset. It is divided into two parts:

*Category tiles (top)*::
Visual cards representing each asset category. Click a tile to filter the asset list
below to that category. Available categories include:

* Laptop
* Parking
* Book
* Desk
* Meeting room
* IT equipment

*Asset list (bottom)*::
A table showing all assets in the selected category, with the following columns:

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Column | Description

| *ID* | Unique identifier for the asset
| *Naziv* (Name) | The name of the specific asset (e.g. MacBook Pro 16)
| *Status* | Whether the asset is currently active (Aktivno) or inactive (Neaktivno)
| *Odobrenje* (Approval) | Indicates whether manager approval is required for this asset
| *(Action button)* | Blue *Rezerviraj* (Book) button to initiate a reservation

|===

=== Filtering by Category

. On the *Rezervacije* page, click any category tile at the top of the page.
. The asset list below will update to show only assets belonging to that category.
. The selected category name appears as the heading above the asset list.
. To clear the filter and show all assets, click the *Poništi filtere* (Reset filters) button
  in the top-right of the asset list area.

=== Filtering by Date

Use the *Datum* (Date) date picker above the asset list to filter assets by a specific date.
This shows only assets that are available on the selected day.

=== Searching for an Asset

Use the *Pretraži imovinu...* (Search assets...) search box in the top-right of the asset
list to search by asset name. Results update as you type.

NOTE: You can combine category filtering with the search box and date picker at the same time.

== Making a Booking

=== Step 1 — Find the Asset

. Navigate to *Rezervacije* in the left sidebar.
. Click the category tile that matches the type of asset you need
  (e.g. *Laptop* to book a laptop).
. Review the asset list. Check the *Status* column — only assets with status *Aktivno*
  can be booked.
. Optionally use the date picker or search box to narrow your results.

=== Step 2 — Open the Booking Calendar

image::Reserve.png[Booking calendar — MacBook Pro 16, May 2026]

. Click the *Rezerviraj* (Book) button next to the asset you want to reserve.
. A dedicated booking page will open, showing:
  * The asset name and its current status badge (e.g. *ACTIVE*)
  * Its location (e.g. *Location: Office 3*)
  * A monthly calendar view showing the current month
  * A *Datum* date picker at the top
  * Navigation arrows to move between months
  * A *today* button to jump back to the current date

=== Step 3 — Select a Date

. Use the calendar to find the date you want to book the asset.
. Today's date is highlighted on the calendar.
. Click the *Datum* date picker field above the calendar and enter or select your
  desired date, or click directly on a date in the calendar grid.

NOTE: Dates that are already booked by another user will appear marked on the calendar.
You cannot select an already-booked date.

=== Step 4 — Confirm the Booking

. Once you have selected your date, click the *Book* button in the top-right corner
  of the booking page.
. Your reservation will be submitted.

NOTE: For some asset categories, a manager approval may be required before the booking
is confirmed. In that case, your booking status will initially show as *Na čekanju*
(Pending) until your manager reviews it. You will be notified once a decision is made.

== Managing Your Bookings

=== Viewing the Categories Page

image::Categories.png[Asset categories list — Kategorije imovine]

The *Kategorije* (Categories) page shows all defined asset categories in a table with
the following columns:

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Column | Description

| *Naziv* (Name) | The category name (e.g. Laptop, Parking, Desk)
| *Opis* (Description) | A short description of the category
| *Period rezervacije* | The booking period unit for this category: Dan (Day), Sat (Hour), or Mjesec (Month)
| *(Actions)* | Eye icon to view details; pencil icon to edit (Admin only)

|===

Regular employees can view categories. Creating or editing categories requires the
*Admin* role.

=== Viewing Your Account Information

image::AccountInfo.png[Account information — Podaci o računu]

Click your name at the bottom of the left sidebar to open the *Podaci o računu*
(Account Information) page. This page displays two cards:

*Profile card (left)*::
Shows your personal details:

* ID
* First name (*Ime*) and last name (*Prezime*)
* Username (*Korisničko ime*)
* Email (*E-mail*)
* A *Promijeni lozinku* (Change password) button

*Work details card (right)*::
Shows your organisational details:

* Role (*Uloga*) — e.g. ADMIN or EMPLOYEE
* Status — e.g. ACTIVE
* Department ID (*Odjel*)
* Manager email (*E-mail voditelja*)
* Notes (*Napomene*)

=== Changing Your Password

. Open the *Podaci o računu* page by clicking your name in the sidebar.
. In the Profile card, click the *Promijeni lozinku* (Change password) button.
. Follow the on-screen instructions to set a new password.

NOTE: Your password must meet the minimum security requirements set by your
IT department. Contact IT support if you are locked out of your account.

image::NewCategory.png[Add new category modal — Dodaj novu kategoriju]

. A modal dialog will appear with the following fields:

[cols="1,1,2", options="header"]

| Field | Required | Description

[cols="1,1,2", options="header"]

| 🟡 Yellow | *Na čekanju* | Pending — awaiting manager approval

|===

== Troubleshooting and FAQ

=== I cannot log in

*Q: I entered my username and password but the login fails.*

A: Check the following:

* Make sure your Caps Lock key is off — passwords are case-sensitive.
* Confirm you are using your Maurer corporate username (not your email address).
* If you have recently changed your corporate password, use the new password here as well.
* If the problem persists, contact your IT helpdesk to reset your account.

---

*Q: I see "Nemate račun? Registrirajte se ovdje." — should I register?*

A: Only if you do not yet have an account in the system. Most employees are
pre-provisioned by the administrator. Try logging in first. If that fails, contact
your IT department rather than self-registering.

=== I cannot find the asset I am looking for

*Q: The asset list is empty or does not show what I need.*

A: Try the following:

* Click *Poništi filtere* (Reset filters) to clear any active category filter.
* Clear any text you have typed in the search box.
* Clear the date filter by removing the selected date.
* The asset may be listed as *Neaktivno* (Inactive) — inactive assets cannot be booked.
  Contact your manager if you believe an asset should be active.

=== The Book button is not working

*Q: I click Rezerviraj but nothing happens.*

A: Ensure the asset status is *Aktivno*. Inactive assets display the Rezerviraj
button but the booking page may indicate the asset is unavailable. If the issue
continues, try refreshing the page or using a different browser.

=== My booking shows "Na čekanju" (Pending)

*Q: I made a booking but it says Pending. Is it confirmed?*

A: Pending means the asset category requires manager approval. Your booking is
submitted and reserved, but it is not fully confirmed until your manager approves it.
You will receive a notification when a decision is made. Contact your manager directly
if approval is taking longer than expected.

=== I cannot see the Korisnici or Kategorije menu items

*Q: The Users and Categories sections are missing from my menu.*

A: These sections are only accessible to users with the *Admin* role. If you believe
you need admin access, contact your system administrator.

=== I want to cancel a booking

*Q: How do I cancel a booking I have already made?*

A: Navigate to your bookings via *Korisnici → Rezervacije* (if you are an admin viewing
your own record) or by contacting an administrator who can cancel it on your behalf.
Self-service cancellation from the reservation calendar may be available — select the
booked date on the asset's calendar and look for a cancel option.

=== The page looks different from this manual

*Q: The language on screen is Croatian but I need English.*

A: Click the flag icon (🇭🇷) in the top-right corner of any page and select your
preferred language from the dropdown.

*Q: The screen is dark and hard to read.*

A: Click the 🌙 (moon) icon in the top-right corner to toggle between dark mode and
light mode.

== Glossary

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Term | Definition

| *Asset (Imovina)* | Any bookable workplace item or space managed by the system, such as a laptop, desk, parking spot, meeting room, or IT equipment.

| *Booking / Reservation (Rezervacija)* | A time-bound claim on an asset made by an employee, preventing others from using it during that period.

| *Category (Kategorija)* | A grouping of similar assets (e.g. Laptop, Parking, Meeting room). Each category has a defined booking period unit (day, hour, or month).

| *Booking period (Period rezervacije)* | The unit of time used for reservations in a given category. Can be Dan (Day), Sat (Hour), or Mjesec (Month).

| *Status — Aktivno* | An asset that is currently available and can be booked.

| *Status — Neaktivno* | An asset that is currently unavailable for booking (e.g. under maintenance).

| *Approval (Odobrenje)* | Some asset categories require a manager to approve a booking before it is confirmed. These bookings start in Pending status.

| *Na čekanju (Pending)* | A booking that has been submitted but is awaiting manager approval.

| *Odobreno (Approved)* | A booking that has been confirmed, either automatically or by a manager.

| *Otkazano (Cancelled)* | A booking that was cancelled by the employee or administrator.

| *Odbijeno (Rejected)* | A booking that a manager declined to approve.

| *Dovršeno (Completed)* | A booking whose reservation period has ended.

| *Role (Uloga)* | The permission level of a user account. EMPLOYEE can browse and book assets. ADMIN can also manage users, categories, and view reports.

| *Department (Odjel)* | The organisational unit a user belongs to, identified by a numeric ID.

| *Manager email (E-mail voditelja)* | The email address of the user's direct manager, used for approval notifications.

| *QR code booking* | A feature of the mobile app that allows employees to scan a QR code on a physical asset to quickly book it on the spot.

|===
